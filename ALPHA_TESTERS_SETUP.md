# 📱 Alpha Testing Setup - Numa Health App

## 🚀 Quick Start for Alpha Testers

### What You'll Test:
- ✅ **Camera functionality** (main MVP feature)
- ✅ **Meal photo capture and analysis**
- ✅ **AI-powered nutrition analysis in Hebrew**
- ✅ **User onboarding and profile setup**
- ✅ **Recent meals tracking**

---

## 📲 Setup Instructions

### Step 1: Install Expo Go
1. Download **Expo Go** from the App Store
2. Create a free Expo account (or login if you have one)

### Step 2: Join Testing
1. **Get QR Code**: Gil will share the QR code with you
2. **Scan Code**: Open Expo Go and scan the QR code
3. **Launch App**: Numa Health App will load on your phone

### Step 3: Test the App
1. **Sign Up**: Create a new account with your email
2. **Complete Onboarding**: 
   - Select gender
   - Enter age, height, weight
   - Choose your health goal
3. **Take Meal Photos**: 
   - Tap "Start Tracking" 
   - Use camera to photograph meals
   - Wait for AI analysis
4. **View Results**: Check nutrition info and tips in Hebrew

## 🧪 What to Test

### Core Features (MVP):
- [ ] **Camera Works**: Can take clear meal photos

## 🐛 How to Report Issues

### Quick Bug Report:
**Send to Gil via WhatsApp/Email:**
1. **What happened**: Brief description
2. **What you expected**: What should have happened
3. **Screenshot**: If possible
4. **Device**: iPhone model and iOS version

### Example:
```
Bug: Camera won't open
Expected: Camera should open when I tap "Take Photo"
Device: iPhone 12, iOS 17.1
Screenshot: [attached]
```
